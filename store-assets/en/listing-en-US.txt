=== APP NAME (max 30) ===
Simple Currency Calculator

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80) ===
Convert as you calculate. 160+ currencies. Works offline once rates are saved.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000) ===
Simple Currency Calculator does one thing well: it converts while you calculate.

CALCULATE AND CONVERT AT ONCE
Type an expression like 1,500 + 3,500 and watch both the running total and its converted value update as you go. No switching apps, no copying numbers between them. Tap the two currency chips at the top to flip the direction instantly.

A DEDICATED CONVERTER
Enter an amount, pick your two currencies, and read the result together with the exact rate used and the date it was published.

FAVORITES AT A GLANCE
Star the currencies you check often and see them all against a single base amount in one grid.

WORKS OFFLINE ONCE RATES ARE SAVED
Open the app once while you are online and the rates are saved on your device. After that it keeps converting on a plane or in a dead zone, using the last rates it downloaded. The first download needs an internet connection, so open the app before you fly. The converter and favorites screens show the date of the rates, so you know how fresh they are.

LIGHT AND DARK
Follows your system theme, or pick one yourself in Settings.

WHERE THE RATES COME FROM
Exchange rates come from ExchangeRate-API and are quoted against the Korean won. They are market rates updated once a day, so they barely change on weekends when currency markets are closed. Conversions between two non-KRW currencies are calculated through the won.

This is a good fit if your currency pairs involve the Korean won. If you need intraday rates, this may not be the right tool for you.

SUPPORTED CURRENCIES (160+)
Major currencies such as KRW, USD, JPY, EUR, CNY, GBP, HKD, TWD, VND, THB, SGD, PHP, AUD, CAD, CHF and NZD, plus more than 140 others. Scroll the list and jump straight to any letter with the A–Z index.

Rates are provided for reference only and will differ from the rate your bank or card issuer applies.

Available in English and Korean.
